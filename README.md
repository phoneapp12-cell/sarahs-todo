# Sarah's To-do

A to-do list for Sarah, shared with Shane's My App. Open https://phoneapp12-cell.github.io/sarahs-todo/ and tap **Install app** (Android/Chrome), or on iPhone tap Share, then **Add to Home Screen**.

Her to-dos are saved on her phone and shared through a small relay, so they also show on Shane's to-do list (with a pink S). Ticks and edits go both ways. Shane's own to-dos are not shown here.
